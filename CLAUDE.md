## Land bank maps (Oct 12, 2026)

Tim's hybrid: one tool, two kinds of map.
- **Question map** (as before): the coalition writes the question; a vote means yes. One number of votes per member.
- **Land bank map**: three fixed answers matching the City of Cleveland Land Bank's programs, in this order: **Open space, Development, Yard expansion** (`CHOICES` in `index.html`; keys `open_space`, `development`, `yard_expansion` are stored, labels can change). Question defaults to "What should each lot become?" (editable). Answers can't be renamed or added to, so every land bank map is comparable lot by lot with every other. Votes per member set per answer; 0 leaves that answer off the map. "Start a map" selects land bank first (Tim, Oct 12).
- **Why:** if the city, a CDC, a council office and resident groups each make a land bank map, their votes line up lot by lot; groups see where they agree, and the city's programs stay the shared reference. City authority = what the city publishes (which lots, which fields). A city field like "recommended use" would show in City record automatically.
- **Voting, both kinds (Tim, Oct 12; replaces Oct 4's tap-votes-at-once; superseded Oct 14 by select-then-tap-a-color):** first tap opens a lot; each tap after that moves your vote to the next answer, then to no vote (question maps: yes → no vote). Skips answers with no votes left; if nothing is left: "You've used all your votes. Tap one of your lots to change it." No take-back button. Optimistic: screen changes on each tap, saved `CONFIG.saveDelay` (800 ms) after the last tap on that lot as one write; saves on one lot go out in order; live refresh is skipped (and its results dropped) while a save is pending or in flight; pending saves flush on route change, page hide and "Not you?". On-site maps check location at save time; off-site taps revert with the distance.
- **One box** (all maps; superseded Oct 14 by the bottom bar, card and ⋯): title link home + About/Download; question; turnout ("Steward · 4 of 25 members · 91 votes on 55 lots"); open lot (address, parcel, ×); the key table (rows = answers with swatch; columns All lots / Yours in Mine view, This lot when open with your vote bold ✓, Left for members with "–" if the answer is off); one note line ("Tie · Darker: more votes"); first-timer hint until the first vote; City record folded (stays open while voting); "On other maps" only when there's something; invite code entry or "Member 7 · group label · Not you?"; Everyone/Mine and Map/List. Top-left 380 px on desktop, bottom (max 62% height) on phones. No success toasts; toasts only for errors.
- **Map look:** Esri World Imagery at `CONFIG.imageryOpacity` (0.5) over white, saturation −0.3; only OpenFreeMap Positron symbol (street name) layers on top; lots have a faint dark casing under a white outline; unvoted lots white at `CONFIG.emptyOpacity` (0.2). Land bank lots take the leading answer's ramp (5 steps vs. the most-voted lot), grey for ties; question maps keep the lilac ramp. Mine view: your lots in your answer's step-4 color. List chips show total votes in the lot's map color; sorts: most votes, most of each answer (land bank), A to Z.
- **Comparing maps:** "On other maps" lists every other map with votes on the open lot: land bank maps as "Ward 7 office · Development 3 · Open space 1" (steward, or question if none), question maps as question + votes. Each links to `#/m/<id>?lot=<parcel>`.
- **Code groups:** codes are issued in labeled groups ("Hough training, Oct 12"); labels are public (turnout by group on the map's About page); hint says no names. Invite cards show the group and the per-answer votes.
- **Downloads:** votes CSV columns `map, question, parcel, label, answer, member, group, voted_at, on_site` (answer "yes" on question maps). Land bank maps also "Totals by lot (CSV)": parcel, label, open_space, development, yard_expansion, total, leading.

## The map screen (Oct 14, 2026)

Tim's spec: a clean interface people can't get lost in; no meta language or explanatory text; color and behavior explain it. The only persistent UI is the map, the bottom bar, and (with a lot selected) the lot's card. No pop-ups, modals, onboarding, or progress counters.
- **Voting:** tap a lot to select it (pink outline with white halo, card opens). Tap a color square to vote. Tapping the square of the lot's current vote takes it back and refunds it; another square moves the vote and refunds the first. A square at 0 can't take new votes or switches: it shakes, no message. Removing a vote always works. Same in Mine and Everyone. Not signed in: tapping a square shakes the invite code bar and focuses it. Tapping empty map, swiping the card down, or Escape clears the selection; no close buttons. Saving unchanged (optimistic, `CONFIG.saveDelay`, one write per lot; errors such as off-site come back as a toast and the vote reverts).
- **Min zoom:** `CONFIG.selectZoom` (15). Below it a tap doesn't select: the map eases in on the tap (to zoom 16.5) and a "Zoom in to vote" pill fades in and out.
- **Opening a map (Tim, Oct 14):** fits to the lots that already have votes (`fitVoted`), so the first votes (usually the steward's) quietly set the group's area and its first recommendations; no boundary setting. With 10+ voted lots the outer 5% each way is trimmed so a few far strays don't stretch it; max zoom 17.5; padded clear of the bar. Deep links (`?lot=`) still win. No votes yet: the whole inventory, or the voter's location on phones.
- **Bottom bar** (one row, max 5 tap targets, fits 320 px): one square per answer (3 on land bank maps, 1 on question maps) filled with the answer's color (`ramp[3]`) showing votes left (the per-member budget before joining). Dimmed with no lot selected (they're the key); lit when a lot is selected; the lot's current vote gets a ring. Then the Mine/Everyone icon (one person / two people; shows the current view) and ⋯.
- **Invite code bar:** slim bar under the bottom bar until this device has a code for the map; then it goes and identity moves to ⋯ ("Member 7 · group · Sign out").
- **⋯ menu:** question, steward, votes each, on-site rule, turnout, color key with labels (land bank); List of lots / Show the map; About this map; Download votes; All maps; Member + Sign out. Closes on outside tap or Escape. On phones it hides the card while open.
- **Card:** phones: compact strip above the bar: address (parcel number if the lot has no street address), your vote (swatch + answer), More/Less, and the next-nearby arrow. Expanded: nearby label or parcel number, this lot's votes per answer, city record, other maps with votes on the lot. Wide screens (≥900 px): panel top-left, always expanded. Hint "Tap a color to vote." until this browser's first vote (`plvt:voted-once` in localStorage), never after.
- **Next nearby** (arrow, swipe left, or → key): selects the closest lot you haven't voted on, skipping lots already stepped past with it this visit. One lot at a time.
- **Views:** default Mine everywhere. Mine: unvoted lots are bare outlines (no fill), your lots solid in your answer's color. Everyone: each lot in its leading answer's color (grey tie), opacity by agreement: the leading answer's votes in five steps vs. the lot with the most agreement on the map (`AGREE` opacities 0.4–0.92); unvoted lots faint white. Dots below zoom 15 follow the same rules.
- **Feedback:** the lot flashes in the vote's color with a spreading ring (`flash` source), the square's number ticks down or up, `navigator.vibrate(10)` where supported. All motion off with reduced motion.
- **Other:** locate button top-right; map credits folded behind the (i) (top-left on phones, bottom-left on wide screens); the list view lives under ⋯ and keeps its search and sorts; double-tap zoom is back on (taps no longer vote).
- **Tested (prototype, Playwright + SwiftShader, 1,536-lot fixture):** default Mine; 5 targets; dimmed/lit squares; min zoom hint; no-code shake; join with a messy code; vote, switch, take back, counts and ticks; empty bucket blocks new votes and switches but not removal; saved record = screen; 160 random taps keep counts = budget − votes and the record = the tally; next nearby (no repeats, nearest); Everyone/Mine paint; More; swipe down and swipe left; tap empty map; menu, list, sign out; reload keeps counts, hint never returns; question map with one square; on-site far (toast, revert) and near; layouts at 375×760, 320×568, 812×375 landscape, 700×800, 1280×800 (no sideways scroll, lot kept clear of the card and bar). Live mode not re-run; Store calls are unchanged.
- **Open:** on question maps the question is only in ⋯ (and the square's label for screen readers). Tim's call pending: default to Everyone (or Leader) until a member's first vote, so newcomers see the recommendations.

## Leaders and faster voting (Oct 15, 2026)

Tim's spec, building on Oct 14 (same principles: clean screen, no meta language, no pop-ups).
- **Leader:** one member per map whose votes are shown as the map's recommendation under a display name ("Tim (WRLC)"). The leader votes with a leader code like anyone else; same per-member votes; counts as one voter in Everyone; no weighting, no separate tally. Every new map gets a leader code (member 1, code group "Leader") and a leader name (start-a-map field "Leader"; blank → the coalition's name). Maps made before Oct 15: the steward names a leader on the invite codes page ("Make a leader code"), which also renames. The codes page shows the leader code with a "Vote as the leader" link; it's left out of printed invite cards and the member code groups. The leader's first pass sets the area the map opens on (Oct 14 `fitVoted`).
- **Leader note:** the leader can add a one-line note (≤80 characters) to a lot they've voted on: a "Why? (optional)" field in the card's expanded view, saved on Enter or leaving the field, after the vote is saved. Others see it only in the expanded view. Taking the vote back removes the note; changing the answer keeps it. Database: `pv_votes.note`, `pv_set_note` (leader only: `not_leader`, `no_vote`, `bad_note`).
- **Card row "who says what":** chips in the answer's color: City · leader's name · You. A source with no answer on the lot shows nothing. The leader sees only their name chip (it's their vote). Hint "Tap a color to vote." sits after the chips until the first vote.
- **City chip:** a hook, off until the city publishes a recommendation. `CONFIG.cityRecommendation`: `field` (null = any field whose name or alias mentions "recommend") and `values` (city wording, lowercase → answer key: open space / green space → open_space, yard expansion / side yard / side lot → yard_expansion, development / redevelopment / housing → development; answer labels also match). Land bank maps only; unknown or blank values show nothing. The current city layer has no such field, so no City chip appears.
- **Three views:** the view icon cycles Mine (person) → Leader (flag) → Everyone (two people); Leader is skipped on maps without one. Each switch shows a pill with the view's name (the leader's name for Leader) for 1 s. Leader view: the leader's lots solid in their answer's color (like Mine) with a dashed dark edge (`lead-line` layer); others bare outlines. Default stays Mine. The list view follows the view.
- **Sticky choice:** long-press a square (phones, 450 ms) or double-click it (mouse) to hold that color: a white inner ring, an outer ring in its color, and a dot. While held, each lot tapped is selected and gets that color; a lot already that color has its vote taken back; a lot with another color switches (refund). Tap the held square to let go; Esc also lets go. When the held color hits 0 it lets go and shakes. Holding needs an invite code (else the code bar shakes) and votes left. On a mouse, the first click of the double-click still votes the selected lot (the second click is ignored). Double-tap on a phone is not a hold.
- **Keys (desktop):** 1/2/3 vote the selected lot with that answer (same rules as the squares; 1 on question maps), N or → next lot nearby, Esc clears the lot and lets go. Listed on the map's About page under "Faster voting" (with the hold gesture); no on-screen legend.
- **Downloads:** votes CSV gains `leader` (yes) and `note`; codes CSV gains `leader`.
- **Samples (prototype):** Hough's sample map has leader "Tim (WRLC)" with a first pass on its hot lots, three with notes; Ward 7's leader is "Ward 7 staff".
- **Tested:** SQL as anon on local Postgres: migration from the Oct 12 schema with an existing map and votes, rerun, old 8-argument `pv_create_map`, leader code at creation, leader votes and notes (member can't, no vote, too long, change keeps, take back drops), `pv_leader`, `pv_codes` leader flag, `pv_record` leader/note, `pv_set_leader` (bad key, blank, too long, create, rename keeps code), no direct table writes. Prototype (Playwright): leader votes and notes load; chips and their colors; note only in More; City chip with a fixture recommendation field (mapped values only, right colors) and never without it; question map has no City chip; view cycle and fading label; Leader view paint; long-press hold, held taps (vote, take back, switch with refund), auto-let-go and shake at 0, tap to let go; leader signs in with their code, sees one chip, saves a note; desktop keys 1/2/3/N/Esc, double-click hold, held clicks; About lists shortcuts; 300 mixed random actions (keys, holds, held taps, Esc) and 160 random taps keep counts = budget − votes and the record = the screen; Oct 14 suites (flows, extent, on-site, layouts 320–1280) still pass. Live against local Postgres with two browsers: map made with a leader through the page, leader link, keys + double-click hold + held clicks saved as member 1, note saved, member's map opens on the leader's lots, member sees chip and note, member vote saved, leader's change picked up by the member within the 20 s refresh, record has leader and note.

## Purpose

Cleveland's public vacant land has no market, so nothing signals what it should become. This tool builds a standing public record of what organized groups of people want, to hold those in power accountable and give everyone a voice. It records votes. Interpretation happens in meetings and conversations.

Home page description (Tim, Oct 4): lead with the democratic intent (information sharing, coalition building, dialogue, finding common ground), not the "no market" framing. Current text: "Public land belongs to all of us. This is a shared place for neighbors, block clubs, and coalitions to say what they want it to become. Each group asks its own question, members vote one lot at a time, and every vote is public. Where maps overlap, groups find each other, start conversations, and find common ground."

## The design (Tim's spec, Oct 4, 2026)

- **The land.** Every map is the city's full land bank inventory, pulled from the city's open data portal. The city decides what's published. When a parcel enters or leaves the inventory, every map updates, and votes on parcels that leave stay in the record. Coalitions don't pick lots.
- **The record.** Votes are stored outside the city, keyed by parcel, with the map and a timestamp. Anyone can view and download them. There's no site admin.
- **Maps.** A coalition creates a map and sets its terms:
  - The kind: a question map (the question defines what a vote means) or a land bank map (the city's three programs as fixed answers; see above).
  - The vote budget: how many votes each member gets (artificial scarcity, so the map shows where members agree most).
  - Invite codes, one per member. They define membership, prevent repeat voting, and show turnout (issued vs. used).
  - On-site voting, optional: a vote counts only if cast near the lot.
  The terms are shown on the map, and the coalition's steward is responsible for them.
- **Voting.** One parcel at a time, with the city's own data for it, blanks included. Location centers the map on the voter, and the voter picks the lot. Working through the whole inventory with a few votes is slow on purpose. Votes are public as they're cast, with an Everyone/Mine toggle. Lots darken as agreement grows, and an address list view covers lots without addresses. One person can vote on any map they have a code for. One vote per lot per map.
- **Connections between maps.** The parcel card lists the other maps where that lot got votes, with each map's question and vote count, linked to its public view. That's how maps are compared and how coalitions find each other.
- **Privacy.** No personal data is stored. On-site voting records a yes/no flag, never coordinates.
- **Removed:** bulk and GeoJSON upload, lot selection at map creation, green/grey categories, map overlay, phone verification (shelved), the "Ballot" name.
- **Still open:** assemblages (groups of adjacent lots); likely left out.
- **Data Day demo:** printed invite cards at the door, on-site voting off, the room votes as one coalition.

## How the app works

One file, `index.html`. Plain HTML/CSS/JS, MapLibre GL 4.7.1 from jsdelivr, supabase-js 2 from jsdelivr (live mode only), qrcode-generator 1.4.4 from cdnjs (loaded only when printing invite cards). No build step.

- **Pages** (hash routes): home `#/` (purpose, a box that takes an invite code or a steward key, list of maps, start a map, download everything); start a map `#/new`; invite codes `#/m/<id>/codes` (steward only; groups, add codes to a group, print cards per group or all); about a map `#/m/<id>/about` (terms, turnout by code group, downloads, link to invite codes); a map `#/m/<id>` (optionally `?lot=<parcel>` to open a lot); `#/join/<code>` (what an invite card's QR code opens: joins and goes straight to the map).
- **Superseded by land bank maps (Oct 12):** the separate top card, member bar, parcel card, legend, "Vote for this lot"/"Take back your vote" buttons, tap-votes-at-once, and the gold selection outline (now pink, since gold is yard expansion). The notes below on those are kept for history.
- **Live updates:** an open map re-reads the tally, turnout, and your votes every 20 s (`CONFIG.refreshSeconds`) while on screen.
- **Invite cards** carry the code, the terms, the page address, and a QR code for `#/join/<code>`. Only unused codes are printed.
- **Starting a map:** question, coalition/steward name, votes per member, number of invite codes, on-site on/off. Creating it shows a **steward key** (three words, shown once) and the codes. The steward key is the only way back to the codes page, where the steward can print invite cards (unused codes only), download codes as CSV, and add more codes. There's no other admin.
- **Invite codes:** 8 characters from an unambiguous alphabet, shown as `K7P3-QX9M`; typing is forgiving (case, dashes). Each code is a member number on that map; the public record shows member numbers, never codes. A code counts as "used" once it has cast a vote. A code entered on another map's page takes you to its own map.
- **Map view:** top card with the question, steward, terms (votes per member, turnout, total votes, on-site rule), Everyone/Mine, Map/List, the darkness key, and Download votes. Bottom card: invite code box, or "Member 7 · 3 votes left of 5" with a budget bar and "Not you?". A locate button centers on the voter (automatically on phones when a map opens); the voter's position is shown as a blue dot and never saved.
- **Parcel card** (tap a lot): label, parcel number, this map's vote count and darkness, the vote button, the city's record (every field the layer publishes, with the layer's own field names; empty values show as "Blank"), and other maps with votes on this lot. On phones the card is a bottom sheet; the top card shrinks to the question and the map pans so the lot stays visible.
- **Tap to vote** (Tim, Oct 4): a member taps a lot and the vote is cast at once; the card opens showing it. Tapping a lot you voted for takes the vote back. Out of votes: the card says so and nothing is cast. Not a member: the card opens with the invite code prompt. A double tap counts once, and double-tap zoom is off because taps are votes. The card keeps "Take back your vote" and, for lots opened from the list or another map, "Vote for this lot". On on-site maps the phone's location is checked against the lot (within 100 m of its center, `CONFIG.onsiteMeters`); off-site votes are refused with the distance, and only `onsite: true/false` is stored.
- **Darkness:** five steps relative to the most-voted lot on that map (`RAMP`, light lilac to near-black). Mine view: your lots in ink, others faint.
- **List view:** every lot, searchable by street, address, or parcel number, sorted by most votes or A to Z, 200 rows at a time; tapping a row opens it on the map.
- **Lots:** read live from the city's layer with all fields (`outFields=*`), 9 parallel requests of 2,000. Saved in IndexedDB keyed by the city's edit date, so later visits load in well under a second. Labels for lots without addresses: "Lot on Canal Rd", "Next to 1218 E 79th St", else "Parcel 101-31-002".
- **Saving:** all through `Store` (maps, map, create, codes, addCodes, findByKey, join, vote, unvote, tally, mine, lotMaps, record). Tallies are `Map(parcel -> [votes per answer])`; your votes `Map(parcel -> answer)`, `"yes"` on question maps. Old prototype records (no kind) load as question maps. `RemoteStore` calls the `pv_*` functions; `LocalStore` (prototype) keeps one JSON record in localStorage (`plvt:prototype:v1`). Codes this device has entered: `plvt:my-codes:live` (or `plvt:my-codes` in the prototype).
- **Prototype tools** (bottom of home, `?prototype` only): add sample maps with invented votes (two land bank maps sharing lots, Hough with two code groups and Ward 7 office, plus an on-site question map), download every vote as CSV, save/load the whole record as JSON, erase everything.
- **Limits:** the on-site check happens on the voter's phone; the database only receives yes/no, so a determined person could fake it. Invite codes are 8 characters from 31 (about 850 billion combinations), and wrong codes and keys are rate-limited per network address.

## Data source

The City of Cleveland land bank inventory ("Land Bank Lots"):
`https://services3.arcgis.com/dty2kHktVXHrqO8i/arcgis/rest/services/City_Landbank/FeatureServer/0`
- Definition query `(isCityLandBank = 1) AND (landBankHoldAreas IS NULL)`; 16,437 lots as of the city's Sep 27, 2026 edit (Residential 16,392, Commercial 45).
- Max 2,000 records per request, paged by `OBJECTID`; CORS open; works from a file opened on disk.
- Fields: `parcelpin` (Parcel Number), `par_addr_all` (Parcel Address), `total_legal_front` (Legal Frontage), `total_square_ft` (Parcel Square Feet), `cityLandBankType` (City Land Bank Type). A street number of 0 means no address.

## The database (schema.sql)

- `pv_parcels` (the city's lots, synced hourly by `pv_sync_city`, archived not deleted), `pv_sync`, `pv_maps` (question, steward, budget, on-site, sha-256 of the steward key, member counter), `pv_codes` (plain codes, readable only through the steward key), `pv_votes` (map, parcel, member number, time, on-site flag), `pv_history` (every vote and take-back), `pv_attempts`.
- Public can read `pv_parcels`, `pv_sync`, `pv_votes`, `pv_history` directly. Maps only through `pv_list_maps` / `pv_get_map` (so the key hash never leaves). Codes and attempts are private.
- Functions the page calls: `pv_list_maps`, `pv_get_map`, `pv_create_map` (generates the id, the three-word steward key, and the codes), `pv_codes` / `pv_add_codes` (steward key), `pv_find_by_key`, `pv_join`, `pv_vote`, `pv_unvote`, `pv_tally`, `pv_mine`, `pv_lot_maps`, `pv_record`. Errors come back as `{"error": "<code>"}`.
- **Land bank maps (Oct 12):** `pv_maps.kind` ('question' default, 'land_bank'), `budgets` jsonb `{"open_space":n,"development":n,"yard_expansion":n}` (budget is null on land bank maps; constraint `pv_maps_terms`); `pv_codes.label` (code group); `pv_votes.choice` (null on question maps); `pv_history.choice` and action 'change'. Signatures: `pv_create_map(question, steward, kind, budget, budgets, members, onsite, label)`, `pv_add_codes(map, key, n, label)`, `pv_vote(code, parcel, choice, onsite)`; new args have defaults, so the Oct 4 page's calls still work. Old signatures are dropped first. `pv_vote` on land bank maps inserts or changes (same answer = no-op), checks each answer's budget (`budget_used`, `choice_off` when 0, `bad_choice`). `pv_tally` returns `[parcel, n]` or `[parcel, open, development, yard]`; `pv_mine` `[parcel, choice]`; `pv_lot_maps` adds kind and `by`; `pv_record` adds kind, choice, label; `pv_map_info` adds kind, budgets, `by` and `groups` (turnout by label). Helpers `pv_answers()`, `pv_by()`, `pv_label()`. The schema also drops the `lp_*` tables/functions when `lp_votes` is empty (otherwise leaves them and says so).
- Rate limits per network address: 30 wrong codes/keys per 10 minutes, 20 new maps per hour.
- `schema.sql` is rerunnable; on a database that still has the Land Ballot tables it renames `lb_parcels`/`lb_sync` to `pv_*` (keeping the 16,437 lots) and drops the rest. The `http` extension needs a 20 s connect timeout for the city's service (set in `pv_city_get`).
